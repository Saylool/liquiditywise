**What are you building, and who is it for?**
LiquidityWise is an independent, educational advisor for Uniswap v3 and v4 liquidity providers, in ten languages. It works out a price range from how far a pair has actually moved, shows where the best-earning liquidity sits (measured on chain from the fees each position earned), lists v4 hooks with their permissions, and shows any public address's positions, with optional Telegram alerts. Every figure is computed by code; the language model that explains it is never allowed to state a number. It is for liquidity providers who do not read English as a first language and who want to check a number rather than trust one.

**How does your project contribute to the Uniswap ecosystem?**
It makes concentrated liquidity easier to understand and safer to act on for people the existing material does not reach: ten languages (the largest readers so far are English and Chinese), six networks including Unichain, and v4 hooks shown with what they can do to a swap. It never asks a wallet to sign or send anything and keeps nothing about its readers, so it adds no risk to Uniswap users. Its method is public on the page (what "smart" means and what the figure leaves out), which others can copy or challenge.
