# The four empty fields of the grant form — what to write, and what only you can decide

## 1. Requested Grant Amount, and how it was reached
Only you know three numbers; with them the amount is arithmetic, not a guess:
- **Hours per week** you will spend on it, and **how many months** (six is a sensible first grant).
- **Your hourly rate.**
- **Monthly running costs** you actually pay today: the RPC / data plan, your share of the server, the domain, model usage (about $1 a week at present).

Breakdown to paste (fill the brackets; leave out any line you will not do):

| Line | How it is counted |
|------|-------------------|
| Engineering: Arbitrum positions source and other chains | [hours] × [rate] |
| Engineering: v4 positions and trend history | [hours] × [rate] |
| Native-speaker review of the ten languages | [languages] × [quote from a reviewer] |
| Independent review of the smart-liquidity method | [one quote] |
| Running costs for the grant period | [months] × [monthly cost] |
| **Total** | sum |

Reviewers read "hours × rate" and quotes as the stronger kind of breakdown (the form says so). Get the two quotes before you submit; a quote you have is worth more than an estimate.

## 2. What the grant is used for, and milestones
Three milestones, each with something a reviewer can open:
1. **Months 1–2 — more of the chain read.** Positions on Arbitrum (it has no source that answers today), and v4 positions. *Evidence:* the smart-liquidity page lists Arbitrum; a test per source.
2. **Months 3–4 — trusted by native readers.** Native-speaker review of all ten languages and a public methodology page that states what "smart" means and what it leaves out. *Evidence:* the reviewers' sign-off and the page.
3. **Months 5–6 — reach.** Telegram range alerts promoted to the communities where the largest readers already are (English and Chinese so far), with the weekly usage report published as counts. *Evidence:* the report's figures at the start and end.

## 3. Your team
Yours to write, in your own words. Facts you can use, all true today: you built the computation layer (every figure is computed, the model never states one), the on-chain measurement, ten languages, monitoring and tests (over 4,000 automated tests), and run the site yourself. Add your name, where you are, and one public link.

## 4. Video link
The tweet's address, once it is posted.
