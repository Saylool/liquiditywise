# Demo video — about two minutes

Screen recording with a voice-over (or captions). One take per scene; no editing tricks. Record at 1280×720 in English, then re-record only the voice-over for other languages: the interface follows the language picker.

**Before recording:** open the pages once so they are warm, and wait until `/smart-money` shows trends (about a day of measurements). Record on desktop; keep one 375 px clip for the phone scene.

| # | Time | On screen | Say |
|---|------|-----------|-----|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | Front page, liquiditywise.com | "Providing liquidity on Uniswap means choosing a price range, and most guides just tell you a number. LiquidityWise works it out from how far the pair has really moved, and explains it." |
| 2 | 0:12–0:40 | Search a pair (e.g. USDC / WETH) → the pool page: range, fees, impermanent loss | "Every figure here is computed by code from the pool's own data and cross-checked. Only then does a language model describe it — and it is never allowed to state a number." |
| 3 | 0:40–1:05 | The same pool with the language picker: switch to Turkish, then Hindi | "Ten languages, each in the words its readers actually use for fees and impermanent loss." |
| 4 | 1:05–1:40 | `/smart-money`: the pairs table, a median range, the trend and the holders | "This page asks which liquidity earned the most. It reads positions from the chain, takes the fees each earned since it last changed, divides by its value, and shows where the top fifth sits. It says what the figure leaves out." |
| 5 | 1:40–1:55 | Holdings page with a public address, then the Telegram bot message | "Paste a public address and see your own positions beside that. An optional bot tells you when one leaves its range. The site never asks a wallet to sign or send anything." |
| 6 | 1:55–2:05 | `/about` page | "Independent and educational. Not affiliated with Uniswap Labs. liquiditywise.com." |

## Rules for the recording
- Say nothing that is not on screen: no "users", "volume" or "partners" unless the weekly report shows them.
- Never show a real person's address other than your own, or a token or key. The smart-money addresses are public, but do not zoom on one.
- Do not claim returns. Point at the method text on the page instead.
